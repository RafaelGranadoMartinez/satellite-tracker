# Asset attribution

## Earth textures

The Earth surface, cloud, normal, and specular textures in
`public/textures/` are from the Three.js examples texture collection:

- `earth_atmos_2048.jpg`
- `earth_clouds_1024.png`
- `earth_normal_2048.jpg`
- `earth_specular_2048.jpg`

Three.js repository: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js

Three.js is distributed under the MIT License:
https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/LICENSE

## Orbital data

Current orbital element data is requested from CelesTrak:
https://celestrak.org/
